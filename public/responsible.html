Responsible Gambling

{{SITE_NAME}} is meant to be a fun side game for adults. Donuts are in-game Minecraft currency with no real-money value: they cannot be bought or cashed out for real money. Even so, gambling-style games can become a habit, and that is worth taking seriously.

18+ only

You must be 18 or older to play. If you are under 18, please leave the Site.

Keep it fun

  • Only play with donuts you are happy to lose. Every bet is final.
  • The house has an edge: over time, you will lose more than you win.
  • Never chase losses or play when upset, tired or under the influence.
  • Set a time and donut limit before you start, and take regular breaks.
  • Never buy donuts, items or accounts with real money to keep playing. That is against our Terms.

Warning signs

  • Playing longer or betting more than you planned.
  • Feeling anxious, irritable or low when you cannot play.
  • Gambling-style play getting in the way of school, work, sleep or friends.
  • Hiding how much you play.
